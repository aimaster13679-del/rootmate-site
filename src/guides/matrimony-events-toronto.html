Matrimony events in Toronto

A NeuMarry Family Mixer is a small, hosted evening where verified singles meet in person over dinner, and parents are welcome. It is the modern version of families meeting families, without the pressure of a formal rishta visit.

Why meet at a Family Mixer

  • See the real person. In one evening you see how someone talks, listens and treats others, which no photo or chat shows.
  • Families meet the same evening. Parents talk in their own lounge, so many rishta questions are answered at the table, not over weeks of calls.
  • Only verified people. Every guest has proven their ID, roots and city, and your host seats you first with your best matches.

For many families, one evening together settles what months of chatting cannot.

The first Family Mixer

  • When: Saturday 28 November 2026, evening
  • Where: a reserved hall at a partner restaurant in the Greater Toronto Area. The address is shared with confirmed guests.
  • Who: fully verified members aged 18 to 38, 30 women and 30 men (the evening is confirmed once 16 women and 16 men reserve)
  • Parents: up to two per member, with their own lounge to meet other parents
  • Dinner: included, from a fixed event menu: vegetarian, Jain or non-vegetarian

How the evening goes

  1. Arrive, check in with your ID and get your name card.
  2. The host welcomes every family and explains the evening.
  3. Three rounds of conversation at separate tables with your top matches.
  4. Dinner at family tables, where parents meet other parents.
  5. More conversations, then everyone goes home.
  6. The next morning, you privately pick who you would like to see again. Mutual picks become matches in the app.

Know who to meet before you go

In the app you can see your match percentage with every member who is going, both ways: how well they fit you and how well you fit them. Your top three matches are seated with you.

The event is confirmed once 16 women and 16 men reserve by 18 November; then you have 48 hours to pay in the app. If it is not confirmed, nobody pays. Full refund up to 48 hours before the event.

Events for every community

Some evenings are for one community, such as Gujarati or Punjabi, so it is quicker to meet someone who shares your language and traditions. Others are open to all communities. Next cities open based on where members join, including Brampton, Mississauga, Vancouver and Calgary.

Questions

Do I have to bring my parents?

No. Parents are welcome but not required. Many members come alone.

Is it speed dating?

No. Conversations are longer, everyone is verified and looking for marriage, families are in the same room, and nobody shares phone numbers on the night. Matches happen afterwards in the app, only when both people say yes.

How do I book?

Install the NeuMarry app, verify your profile, and reserve a free seat in the Events tab.
