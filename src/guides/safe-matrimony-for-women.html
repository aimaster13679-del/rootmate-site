Safe matrimony for women living abroad

Meeting someone new should feel exciting, not risky. NeuMarry is built so that you decide who you talk to, everyone you meet has proven who they are, and you never have to share your number to get to know someone.

Everyone is checked before they can write to you

  • ID and live selfie. Every member uploads a government ID and takes a live selfie. A person on our team compares them.
  • Roots and city. Members also prove where they grew up and where they live now, so the profile you read is the person you meet.
  • Only fully verified members are shown. Nobody can send you an interest or a message before these checks are done.

Your number stays private

Chats happen inside the app. Phone numbers are never shown, and messages that ask for money, bank details or payment apps are stopped before they reach you. Every report is read by a person, usually within 24 hours.

Meet in a safe place, with family if you like

NeuMarry evenings take place at a partner restaurant with a NeuMarry host. Guests sit at separate tables in one shared room, everyone is checked in with a QR ticket at the door, and parents are welcome at Family Mixers. For a first meeting outside an event, the app helps you share your meeting plan with someone you trust.

Fair for everyone

Women and men pay the same prices for events. Each event has the same number of seats for women and for men, so nobody is outnumbered. The first 300 verified women in every country get 3 months of Gold free.

Questions

Can men see my photos straight away?

Full names and photos are shown only inside the app, never on the open web unless you share your own family biodata link. You decide whose interest to accept, and you can block or report anyone at any time.

Do I have to come to events alone?

No. You can bring up to 2 parents to a Family Mixer with their own ticket, and a NeuMarry host is there all evening.

Is NeuMarry only for one community or country?

No. NeuMarry is for people of every country who live away from where they grew up. Events can be open to all communities or for one community.
