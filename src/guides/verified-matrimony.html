Verified matrimony, without fake profiles

Fake profiles and money scams are the biggest worry on matrimony sites. NeuMarry checks every member before they can contact anyone, and keeps your details private until you choose to share them.

Every member is checked in 5 steps

1
Government ID

A passport, national ID, residence card or driving licence. Name, date of birth and photo must match the profile.

2
Live selfie

Taken in the app, not uploaded. We compare it with the ID and the profile photos, so nobody can use someone else’s pictures.

3
Roots document

Proof of the country and region a member comes from, such as a passport, national ID or birth certificate.

4
Proof of the city they live in

A status document for the country (citizenship, residence or a valid permit) and an address document from the last 3 months. This check is renewed every year.

5
Photo review

Every new photo is checked automatically for unsafe content and then by a person before anyone can see it.

Only fully verified members are shown. A profile appears to others only after all five checks are approved. Paused, banned and blocked profiles are never shown. Document images are deleted within 30 days of the check; we keep only the result. Once your ID is approved, your name, date of birth and gender are locked.

Every report is reviewed by a person, and accounts that break the rules are removed and kept out. You can block anyone from their profile or chat, and they will not see you again.
