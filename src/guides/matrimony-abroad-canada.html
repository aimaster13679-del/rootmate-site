Matrimony in Canada for people who grew up elsewhere

If you grew up in India or South Asia and now live in Canada, finding a life partner is a different problem from finding one back home. NeuMarry is a matrimony app built for exactly that: verified people who share your roots, live in your city and think like you about marriage.

Why finding a partner in Canada is hard when you grew up elsewhere

Most matrimony sites were built for people living in India. When you live in Toronto, Brampton or Vancouver, a big site mostly shows you profiles thousands of kilometres away, or people who want to move abroad through marriage. Families back home send biodatas, but the person is in another country with different plans.

At the same time the community here is large and young. Canada's 2021 Census counted 2.57 million South Asians, the country's largest racialized group, with a median age of 32. Nearly half live in the Toronto area. The right person is often close by. The hard part is meeting them.

How NeuMarry is different

  • Matches on roots, city and mindset. We look at where you come from (country, state, language and community), where you live now down to the distance, and your answers to nine short questions about family, careers, faith and where you want to live.
  • Only verified members. Everyone uploads a government ID and takes a live selfie. Our team checks both before anyone can send interests or chat.
  • Private by default. Photos stay hidden until you both say yes. Your phone number, email and ID are never shown to other members.
  • Families included. Parents can create a profile for a son or daughter, and come to hosted Family Mixers with their own ticket.
  • Built for people already settled here. Every profile shows residency (citizen, permanent resident, work permit or study permit) and whether they plan to stay in Canada.

Who joins

NeuMarry is for adults 18 and older who live away from the country they grew up in and want to marry. It is built for software developers, nurses, accountants, students finishing their degrees and business owners, from Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayali, Marathi, Bengali and many other communities, and from every religion. You choose which communities you are open to.

Where we start

The app works anywhere in Canada. In-person events start in the Greater Toronto Area, with the first Family Mixer in Toronto on 28 November 2026. Next cities open where most members join, including Vancouver, Calgary and Ottawa.

Questions

Is NeuMarry only for Indians?

No. It started with Indian and South Asian families in Canada and welcomes every community, religion and country of origin.

Do I need permanent residency to join?

No. Citizens, permanent residents and people on work or study permits can all join. Your residency is shown on your profile so matches know your situation.

Can my parents search for me?

Yes. A parent can create the profile on your behalf. See NeuMarry for parents.
