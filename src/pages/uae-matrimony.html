Matrimony in the UAE

Most people who live in the UAE grew up in another country. NeuMarry helps you meet a verified life partner who lives in the UAE too, wants the same kind of married life, and shares your roots if that matters to you.

Use NeuMarry in the UAE today

The app works in every country. Open neumarry.com/app in any web browser, on your phone or computer, to create your profile, get verified, see your matches and chat. The Android app uses the same account.

Close by, or ready to move

Every profile shows how far away the person lives, so members in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Al Ain see the people nearest to them first. You can also keep the best match first, choose how far away people may live, or change “Where they live” in Filters to see people in another country, or in every country.

Matched on the life you want

More than half of your match % comes from nine questions about married life, starting with where you want to settle after marriage. That matters when you live abroad: some members plan to stay in the UAE, some want to move back home one day, and some are open to both. How matching works.

Families, wherever they live

A parent or family member can create a profile for a son or daughter, and every profile shows who created it. You can share a private family biodata link with parents in any country; it lasts 7 days and you can stop it at any time.

Events

Family Mixer evenings start in Toronto, Canada, on Saturday 28 November 2026. There are no NeuMarry events in the UAE yet; we open events in a city when it has enough members.

Real people only

Every member is checked with a government ID and a live selfie before they can message anyone. Photos stay hidden until you both say yes, and your phone number is never shown. Privacy and safety.

Questions

Can I join if I am not a citizen of the UAE?

Yes. Anyone 18 or older who lives away from the country they grew up in can join. If you want, you can choose to see only citizens and permanent residents in your filters.

Is it free?

Creating a profile, getting verified and seeing your match % with each person is free. NeuMarry Gold shows names and photos and lets you send interests and chat; the price is shown in the app in your local currency before you pay.

Can I meet people in other countries too?

Yes. In Filters, change “Where they live” to another country, or to any country. Also see Matrimony in the UK and Matrimony in the USA.
