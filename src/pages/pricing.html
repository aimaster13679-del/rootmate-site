Free and Gold

Joining NeuMarry is free. You pay only if you want to see who people are and start talking, and only for as long as you choose.

What is free

  • Create your profile and get verified with an ID and a live selfie
  • See how many people fit what you are looking for
  • See your match % with each of them, and the reasons
  • Chat when a Gold member matches with you

What Gold adds

  • Full profiles with names and photos
  • Send interests and chat
  • All your daily picks, and who likes you
  • Browse privately

Questions

Can I cancel Gold?

Yes, at any time. On Android, open Google Play, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel NeuMarry Gold. You keep Gold until the end of the period you paid for.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds for Gold bought on Android follow Google Play’s refund policy.

Is there a free trial?

Everything in the free plan stays free for as long as you like, so you can see your matches and match % before you decide.
