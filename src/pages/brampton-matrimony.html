Questions

Do I have to live in Brampton to join?

No. Anyone 18 or older who lives away from the country they grew up in can join, anywhere. Brampton members simply see the people closest to them first.

Is it free?

Creating a profile, getting verified and seeing your match % with each person is free. NeuMarry Gold, from CA$80 a month, shows names and photos and lets you send interests and chat.

Can my parents create my profile?

Yes. A parent or family member can create a profile for a son or daughter, and every profile shows who created it.
