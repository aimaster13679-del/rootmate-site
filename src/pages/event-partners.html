Cafes and restaurants: three ways members come to you

  • First meetup. Two members who have talked for a day pick your place and a set menu from your page, and book a table for two. They pay you for the food at your place.
  • Family Mulaqat. After a first meetup, both families meet at your family table. You get the date, time, first names and the number of guests.
  • Introduction Evenings. With the Host plan (US$49 a month in the United States, included in Pro) you run small evenings of 12 to 20 verified members of one roots group. You set the dinner price and keep it.

NeuMarry takes no commission from you. Members pay NeuMarry a small booking fee; you never handle it. We share only first names and the number of guests, never phone numbers.

Prices above are for the United States; the price for your country is shown before you pay. You pay once a month on neumarry.com. Nothing renews by itself: when a month ends, you choose to continue or not.

Families never pay to contact you. Every member who sends you a request is a real person, checked with an ID and a live selfie.

How it works

  1. Create your business page: name, city, services and prices. About 5 minutes.
  2. Send your business registration. A person checks it, usually within 24 hours.
  3. Your page shows to families in your city under Event Partners.
  4. Families send a request with the date and number of guests. You reply and agree everything directly with them.

NeuMarry does not take a share of what families pay you. We never hold your money or sign contracts for you.

Questions

What happens after the 14 free days?

Your page stays saved but hidden. Pick a plan to show it again. Nothing is charged automatically, because we never ask for your card during the trial.

Which countries?

Every country where NeuMarry members live. Families see partners in their own city first.

Can I stop at any time?

Yes. Your plan simply ends at the end of the month you paid for.
