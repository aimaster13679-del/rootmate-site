About NeuMarry

NeuMarry is a matrimony app for people who live away from the country they grew up in. It helps you find a life partner for marriage who shares your roots and your outlook, lives in the same city, and is verified as a real person, with your family part of the journey.

Why NeuMarry exists

NeuMarry started when someone close to our founder, living in Canada, struggled to find a life partner from his own background.

Millions of people move abroad to study and work. Many of them want to marry someone from their own background, but the big matrimony sites are built for people living in their home country, and dating apps are built for something else. Someone from your own region might live ten minutes away, and you would never know.

NeuMarry starts from where you live now. Every match shows how far away the person lives, where their family comes from, and how closely you think alike about the things that matter in a marriage.

What makes it different

  • Only verified members. Every member checks in with a government ID and a live selfie before they can send an interest or a message. How verification works.
  • Roots, city and mindset. Matches are based on nine questions about married life, your lifestyle, where you live and where you come from. How matching works.
  • Private by default. Photos stay hidden until you both say yes. Your phone number and email are never shown to other members.
  • Family by your side. Parents can create a profile for a son or daughter and come to Family Mixer evenings with their own ticket.
  • For every country and community. Members choose whether they want someone from their own community or are open to everyone.

Where we are today

NeuMarry opens in Canada in November 2026, starting with the Greater Toronto Area: Toronto, Brampton and Mississauga. The app works in every country, and in-person events open city by city as each city has enough members. You can use NeuMarry now in your web browser. The Android app follows on Google Play, with iPhone after that.

What we are responsible for

We verify members, suggest matches and host events where people can meet. Decisions after an introduction, and any relationship or marriage, belong to the two people and their families. Read our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
