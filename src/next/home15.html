Questions

Is NeuMarry still a matrimony app?

NeuMarry now helps people living abroad with everything around a new life: rooms, jobs, travel for parents, events and friends from their roots.

Who runs the events?

Independent hosts in your city. They pay a plan to host. Every ticket has a QR code, and if the host cancels, the host must return your ticket money.

Is my money safe?

Our own fees are paid on our website with PayPal. For now, event tickets are paid directly to the host in the way shown on your ticket: pay only to those details. Moving-Out Sale payments open later through a licensed payment partner.

Which cities?

Toronto first, then more cities in Canada, the USA, Australia and Germany.
