Questions

Who is NeuMarry for?

For people of every country living away from the country they grew up in, who want marriage with someone who understands both worlds.

Is this a dating app?

No. NeuMarry is only for marriage. There is no swiping, and every member is checked.

Can my parents make my profile?

Yes, with your consent. You still do the ID check yourself, and the profile shows who created it.

Where are rooms, events and friends?

They moved to our separate app NeuAmigo at neuamigo.com.
