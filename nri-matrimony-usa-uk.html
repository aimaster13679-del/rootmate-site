NRI matrimony in the USA, the UK and worldwide

Updated 22 October 2026 · NeuMarry

NeuMarry is for everyone who lives away from the country they grew up in. The app works in every country, and in-person events open city by city, starting in Toronto.

United States

New York, New Jersey, the San Francisco Bay Area and Dallas have large Indian and South Asian communities, many of them on work visas or green cards. On NeuMarry your residency status is part of your profile, so matches understand your situation from the start. New York and New Jersey, the Bay Area and Dallas are on our list of next cities.

United Kingdom

London and Leicester are home to established Gujarati, Punjabi and Tamil families, and to many newer arrivals on work and study visas. London and Leicester are on our list of next cities.

Australia and the Gulf

Sydney, Melbourne and Dubai are also on the waitlist. Members there can join the app and match with people near them and in other countries if they are open to moving.

What is the same everywhere

  • Every member is checked with a government ID and a live selfie.
  • Matches on roots, city and values, with the reasons shown on every profile.
  • Photos hidden until both people accept; your number is never shown.
  • Parents can create a profile on behalf of a son or daughter.

How cities open

We open in-person Family Mixers where the waitlist grows fastest. Join the waitlist on the home page and choose your city; it helps bring events to you sooner.
