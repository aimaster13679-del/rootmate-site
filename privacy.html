Privacy Policy

Version of 28 October 2026 · applies to the NeuMarry apps, this website and NeuMarry events

NeuMarry collects what you share to verify you, find you matches and run events. We never sell it, and you can see, correct or delete it at any time. The person responsible for your data is Swapnil Dinesh Bhagat, Surat, Gujarat, India (contact: support@neumarry.com) ("NeuMarry", "we"), until NeuCoNet Private Limited takes over the Service.

Key points

  • Your phone number, email, ID and exact location are never shown to other members.
  • Your photos stay hidden until you both accept, unless you choose to show them.
  • ID, selfie and document images (proof of your roots, your status and your address) are deleted within 30 days of the check. We keep only the result.
  • We check your phone’s approximate location only if you choose to, once, to confirm your city. We never store the position.
  • Showing your religion and community on your profile is optional.
  • This website counts visits with Google Analytics, without ads. In Europe, the UK and Switzerland it does so only if you accept.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time: Me, then Delete my account. More about deleting your account.

2. How we use it

2.1 We use your data to: create and secure your account; verify you, your roots and the city you live in; suggest matches and calculate match percentages; show your profile to other members under your privacy settings; run chat and events; process payments and refunds; keep members safe and enforce our Terms; send service messages; send marketing only if you agreed; and fix and improve the Service.

2.2 Legal bases. We rely on your consent (for example your religion and community details and marketing), on what is needed to provide the Service you signed up for, on our legitimate interest in keeping the Service safe and free of fraud, and on legal obligations such as tax records. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect what we did before.

2.3 Matching is automated, decisions are yours. Our system ranks profiles and calculates match percentages from your answers and preferences. It does not decide anything with legal effect about you. You can change your preferences, and ask us how your match percentage is worked out.

2.5 Moderation and automatic safety checks. Our team reviews new photos before other members can see them. Before that, each new photo may also be checked automatically for nudity and sexual content by Google Cloud Vision. Google processes the photo in memory only, does not store it and does not use it to train its models; we keep only the result. When you send a message or save your profile, the text is checked automatically for abusive words and for requests for money, bank or payment details, gift cards or money transfers. Text like that is not sent or saved; we keep only a note that it was blocked, in which field and when, not the words. Messages that mention visa help show a safety warning. Our team also reviews profiles and messages that are reported or flagged. We do not read messages for advertising or any other purpose.

5. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, access limited to people who need it, row-level security in our database so each member can reach only what they are allowed to, private storage for photos and ID images, and passwords stored only as salted hashes. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts you at real risk of significant harm, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.
