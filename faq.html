Questions and answers

Everything people usually ask before they join NeuMarry. Can’t find your question? Write to us at the email address at the bottom of this page.

Joining

Who can join NeuMarry?

Anyone 18 or older who lives away from the country they grew up in and is looking for marriage, from any country and community.

Is there an income or salary requirement?

No. NeuMarry has no income, salary or wealth requirement for anyone, men or women. Every member is checked the same way: ID and selfie, roots and the city they live in. Matching is based on roots, values and life plans, not money.

Is NeuMarry a dating app?

No. NeuMarry is a matrimony app for people who want to marry. Matching starts with nine questions about married life.

Which countries does NeuMarry work in?

The app works in every country.

Can I use NeuMarry on the website?

Yes. Open neumarry.com/app in any web browser, on your phone or computer, to create your profile, get verified, see your matches and chat. The Android app uses the same account.

Which languages is the app in?

The app is in English and French.

Can my parents create my profile?

Yes. A parent or family member can create a profile for a son or daughter, and every profile shows who created it. Parents cannot message anyone as you.

Price

Is NeuMarry free?

Joining is free: create your profile, get verified, see how many people fit what you are looking for and your match % with each, and reply when a Gold member writes to you.

What does Gold cost?

In the United States, US$39.99 for 1 month, US$99.99 for 3 months or US$149.99 for 6 months. Other countries see their own price in the app before paying (for example CA$54.99 a month in Canada). Paid once on neumarry.com with PayPal; nothing renews by itself.

Can I cancel Gold?

Yes, at any time. On Android, open Google Play, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel NeuMarry Gold. You keep Gold until the end of the period you paid for.

Matching

How is my match % worked out?

Values 55% (nine questions about married life), lifestyle 15%, your age and height preferences 10%, where you live 10%, and roots 10%. Up to three reasons show next to every match %.

Do I have to match within my community?

No. You choose which communities you are open to, or leave it open to everyone.

Privacy and safety

How do you stop fake profiles?

Before anyone can see a member, a person checks their government ID, a live selfie and their photo. Roots and city are extra badges shown on the profile, and a person reviews every report within 24 hours.

Who sees my photos and details?

Only verified members. Photos stay hidden and only your first name and last initial show until you both accept. Your phone number, email, ID and exact location are never shown.

How do I block or report someone?

Open their profile, tap the three dots, then Block or Report. A person on our team reviews every report within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Me, then Delete my account. You can also follow the steps on our delete account page.
