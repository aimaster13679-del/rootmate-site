Community matrimony in Canada

Updated 22 October 2026 · NeuMarry

For many families, sharing a language, food and festivals matters as much as sharing a city. NeuMarry lets you look for someone from your own community, or be open to everyone, and changes the matches you see as you change your mind.

How community matching works

Your profile records where you come from: country, state, mother tongue and, if you choose, religion and community. Showing religion and community on your profile is optional. In your preferences you pick which communities you are open to, and your match percentage reflects shared roots alongside values and distance.

Gujarati matrimony in Canada

Gujarati families are one of the largest groups in the Greater Toronto Area, especially in Brampton, Mississauga, Scarborough and Markham. NeuMarry was started with Gujarati and Punjabi families in mind, and Gujarati-only Family Mixers are planned alongside open events.

Punjabi and Sikh matrimony in Canada

Brampton, Surrey and Calgary have some of the largest Punjabi communities outside India. Members can filter by Punjabi language and Sikh faith, and see how far each match lives from them.

Tamil, Telugu, Malayali and Kannada matrimony

South Indian professionals across Toronto, Ottawa, Waterloo and Vancouver can match on mother tongue and home state, so a Telugu speaker from Hyderabad sees other Telugu speakers first if they want to.

Marathi, Bengali and Hindi-speaking families

Choose your mother tongue and state, and NeuMarry gives extra weight to shared roots without hiding people who could also be a great fit.

Muslim, Christian and Jain matrimony

Faith can be shown on your profile or kept private. Members who care about faith can filter by it, and dietary choices such as Jain and vegetarian food are respected at every Family Mixer.

Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Sri Lankan and Nepali matrimony

NeuMarry is for everyone who lives away from the country they grew up in. Members from across South Asia, and from many other regions, are welcome.

Open to everyone

Many members are open to more than one community. NeuMarry shows why each match was suggested, such as "Both from Gujarat" or "Both want to stay in Canada", so you can decide what matters most to you.
