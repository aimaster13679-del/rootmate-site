Verified matrimony, without fake profiles

Updated 22 October 2026 · NeuMarry

Fake profiles and money scams are the biggest worry on matrimony sites. NeuMarry checks every member before they can contact anyone, and keeps your details private until you choose to share them.

Every member is checked

  1. Government ID. A passport, residence card or driving licence. Only our team sees it, and the image is deleted within 30 days of the check.
  2. Live selfie. We compare it with the ID and the profile photos, so nobody can use someone else's pictures.
  3. Photo review. Every new photo is reviewed before it appears.

Members without an approved ID and selfie cannot send interests, chat or book events. Once your ID is approved, your name, date of birth and gender are locked so they cannot be changed later.

Your privacy

  • Photos stay hidden until you both say yes, unless you choose to show them.
  • Your phone number, email, ID and exact location are never shown to other members.
  • Showing religion and community is optional.
  • You can pause your profile, block anyone, and delete your account in the app at any time.

Scam protection

Every report is reviewed by a person, and accounts that break the rules are removed and kept out. You can block anyone from their profile or chat, and they will not see you again.

Safety tips for any matrimony site

  • Never send money to someone you have not met, whatever the story.
  • Meet in a public place for the first time, and tell a friend or family member.
  • Keep conversations in the app until you trust the person.
  • Be careful with anyone who avoids video calls or meeting in person.
  • Report anything that feels wrong. We read every report.
