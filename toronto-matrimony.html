Matrimony in Toronto and the GTA

Updated 22 October 2026 · NeuMarry

The Greater Toronto Area is home to more South Asian families than anywhere else in Canada. NeuMarry helps you meet a verified life partner who already lives here, close enough to meet for coffee this weekend and to bring both families together.

Matches by distance, not just by city name

When you set your city, NeuMarry shows how far each match lives from you. Someone in Brampton and someone in Scarborough are both "Toronto" on most sites, but a long drive apart. You can sort matches by the nearest first, or keep the best match first and see the distance on every profile.

Neighbourhoods we cover

Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Markham, Vaughan, Oakville, Milton, Scarborough, Hamilton and Kitchener-Waterloo. Members anywhere else in Ontario and Canada can join too, and choose how far they are willing to travel or move.

Meet in person at a Family Mixer

Many people in Toronto are tired of chatting for weeks with someone they never meet. NeuMarry hosts small evenings where verified members meet in person over dinner, and parents can come along with their own ticket. The first one is on Saturday 28 November 2026. Your community, or everyone

Some members want someone from the same language and traditions; others are open to everyone. On NeuMarry you choose which communities you are open to: Gujarati, Punjabi, Sikh, Tamil, Telugu, Malayali, Marathi, Bengali, Hindi-speaking, Muslim, Christian, Jain, Pakistani, Sri Lankan and more. Safe by design

Every member is checked with a government ID and a live selfie before they can message anyone. Photos stay hidden until you both accept, and your number is never shown. Read how we keep profiles real.
