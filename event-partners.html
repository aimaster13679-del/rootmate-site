Event Partners

Families on NeuMarry plan introductions, family dinners and weddings in the country they live in now. Show them your business, your services and your prices. Try it free for 14 days. No card needed.

Who it is for

  • Restaurants and banquet halls that can host a family dinner or a NeuMarry event
  • Caterers, bakers and sweet shops
  • Photographers and videographers
  • Decorators, florists, DJs and musicians
  • Wedding planners, makeup and henna artists

Prices are in US dollars; your local price is shown before you pay. You pay once a month on neumarry.com. Nothing renews by itself: when a month ends, you choose to continue or not.

Families never pay to contact you. Every member who sends you a request is a real person, checked with an ID and a live selfie.

How it works

  1. Create your business page: name, city, services and prices. About 5 minutes.
  2. Send your business registration. A person checks it, usually within 24 hours.
  3. Your page shows to families in your city under Event Partners.
  4. Families send a request with the date and number of guests. You reply and agree everything directly with them.

NeuMarry does not take a share of what families pay you. We never hold your money or sign contracts for you.

Questions

What happens after the 14 free days?

Your page stays saved but hidden. Pick a plan to show it again. Nothing is charged automatically, because we never ask for your card during the trial.

Which countries?

Every country where NeuMarry members live. Families see partners in their own city first.

Can I stop at any time?

Yes. Your plan simply ends at the end of the month you paid for.
