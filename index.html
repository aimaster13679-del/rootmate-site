Questions

Before you join.

Is NeuMarry free?

Joining is free: create your profile, verify, see how many people fit what you are looking for and your match % with each, and reply when a Gold member writes to you. NeuMarry Gold unlocks names and photos, interests, chat, daily picks and event guest lists, from CA$80 a month. Event tickets are separate and include the meal.

Who can join?

Anyone 18 or older who lives away from the country they grew up in and is looking for marriage. We start in the Greater Toronto Area and open other cities when each has enough members.

Is there an income or salary requirement?

No. NeuMarry has no income, salary or wealth requirement for anyone, men or women. Every member is checked the same way: ID and selfie, roots and the city they live in. Matching is based on roots, values and life plans, not money.

How do you stop fake profiles?

Every member verifies a government ID and a live selfie before they can send interests or messages, and every report is reviewed by a person.

Who sees my photos and details?

Only verified members. Photos stay blurred and only your first name and last initial show until you both accept. Your phone number is never shown unless you both choose to share it. Religion and community are optional.

Can my parents be involved?

Yes. Parents can sign up on your behalf and come to family events with their own ticket. A Family Circle where they can suggest profiles is coming soon. They cannot message anyone as you.

What if an event does not fill?

Nobody pays. Reserving in the app is free, and we ask for payment only after enough people reserve to confirm the event. If it is not confirmed in time, it is cancelled or moved to a new date, and you keep your free reservation.

Can I use NeuMarry on the website?

Yes. Open neumarry.com/app in any web browser, on your phone or computer, to create your profile, get verified, see your matches and chat. The Android app uses the same account.

Can my parents join from another country?

Yes. From Me in the app you can share a private family biodata link: your parents open it in any country, on a phone or computer, without installing anything. The link lasts 7 days and you can stop it at any time. A NeuMarry host can join to make the introductions.

What is NeuMarry responsible for?

We verify members, suggest matches, and host events where you can meet. Decisions after an introduction, and any relationship or marriage, belong to the two people and their families. We do not run criminal background checks, so please follow our safety advice and never send money to anyone you meet.

When does my city open?

Toronto opens first, in November 2026. Join the waitlist from anywhere and choose your city; we open the next cities based on where the waitlist grows fastest.
